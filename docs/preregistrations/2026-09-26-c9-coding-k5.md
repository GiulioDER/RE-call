## Design

`recall_aml` from the candidate tree, a fresh database (`codingcheck_k5_20260926`) on VPS3, the same
frozen AMB Coding screen (196 sessions, 34 prompts), the served C9 variant, no experiment flag or
override set in the environment. It starts after K4 has finished and reads a copy of K4's embedding
cache (content-addressed on text and embedder identity, so vectors are identical and only Voyage
calls are saved). Compared by `report` with K0, K0b, K3 and K4.

## Decision rule, fixed now

N is the noise floor |K0b minus K0| on MRR (0.000 measured).

1. Apparatus failure (below): report, decide nothing.
2. **Keep** if K5 MRR is at least K0's minus (0.02 + N), recall@10 at least 33, recall@100 34, and
   |K5 minus K4| MRR at most 0.02 + N.
3. **Otherwise report** the tasks that moved between K4 and K5 and hold the Coding version until the
   user decides.

Merging #775 and deploying remain the user's decisions and wait for the Textual Full to finish
(user, 2026-09-26: stopped but not finished); K5 decides neither.
