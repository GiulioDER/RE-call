Choosing an agent memory layer

RE-call compared with other memory layers

These projects solve different parts of agent memory, and several of them are further along than RE-call. The useful question is not which is best but what each one does when a remembered fact stops being true, and what it needs to run.

Descriptions of other projects come from their public repositories and documentation, checked on 2026-11-05. They change quickly; follow the links before relying on a detail. The sourced, per-capability record is the prior art comparison.

What happens to a fact that stopped being true

  • RE-call marks it superseded when a newer memory declares that it replaces it, and returns the current one ahead of it. The author declares the replacement; RE-call does not infer it.
  • Graphiti infers the contradiction with an LLM at ingestion and invalidates the older edge rather than deleting it.
  • Mem0, since its 2026 algorithm, adds memories without updating or deleting them on the add path, and handles time at retrieval. Explicit update and delete remain in its API.
  • Letta leaves it to the agent, which rewrites its own memory blocks.
  • A plain vector store returns the stale text whenever it is the nearest match.

What RE-call gives up

  • Coverage of supersession. Declared replacements are precise but rare: on the reference corpus, 2 of 792 memos declared one while 60 closed a decision only in prose. An LLM that infers replacements covers more and is sometimes confidently wrong; RE-call chooses precision.
  • No entity reasoning. There is no knowledge graph of people and relations to reason over, which is where Graphiti is stronger.
  • Abstention costs answers. Out of the box, default retrieval abstained on none of LoCoMo's 446 adversarial questions. The shipped levers raise that to between 0.37 and 0.77 only by refusing a quarter to half of legitimate questions.
  • Lite installs from source for now. The SQLite edition is newer than the published 0.14.0 wheel; see the Lite guide.

The one paired measurement

On LoCoMo's 1,540 answerable questions, with the same GPT-4o reader and judge and matched retrieval budgets, RE-call scored 48.4% judged answer accuracy against Mem0 OSS at 44.4%. RE-call ran this test itself, and the direction depends on the reader: a later Sonnet 4.5 subset reverses it. The method, artifact and limits are on the benchmark scorecard.

RE-call has not been compared head to head with Graphiti, Zep, Letta or LangMem, so this page makes no score claim about them.
